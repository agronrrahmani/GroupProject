About Vanta

Vanta is a modern clothing brand focused on modern fashion, clean design, and Your culture. We create stylish clothing for people who want to show there identity.


BUILT FROM THE OUTSIDE IN.


Founded in London by a small collective of designers, photographers, and musicians, VANTA began with one belief: what you wear should amplify who you are, not define it for you. We design slowly and deliberately. Every silhouette is tested in motion. Every material is selected for how it feels, ages, and performs. No excess. No compromise.
